This Privacy Policy explains how Luxon Neu INC FZE ("Luxon Neu," "we," "us" or "our") handles personal data when you visit https://luxonneu.com/, contact us, discuss a project or receive our business services. Luxon Neu INC FZE is a Free Zone Establishment with Limited Liability registered with the Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone Authority, formation number 4430227 and business licence number 4430227.01. This Policy is intended to operate with the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, where applicable, and other applicable data-protection laws. A project agreement or data-processing agreement may add more specific rules for client data.

01Who we are and our role

For personal data about website visitors, people who contact us, and business contacts, Luxon Neu INC FZE (formation number 4430227; business licence number 4430227.01) is generally the organisation deciding why and how the data is used. Our registered business address is Business Centre, Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates. You can contact us at info@luxonneu.com.

For some client projects, a client decides why personal data is used and instructs us to process it to build, host, maintain or support a system. In those cases, the client may be the controller and we may act as its processor. The written project agreement and, where applicable, data-processing agreement define each party's responsibilities. Please direct rights requests about data controlled by a client to that client; we will assist the client as required by our agreement and law.

02Personal data we may collect

The information we handle depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

  • contact and identity details, such as your name, work email address, telephone number, job title and company;
  • business enquiry and project details, including the information you choose to include in messages, calls, proposals or support requests;
  • account, contract and transaction information if you become a client or supplier;
  • technical information, such as IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, referring page, time and security logs; and
  • information you provide through a website form, event, recruitment channel or other service that links to this Policy.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, government identifiers, health information or other sensitive personal data through an ordinary email or general enquiry unless we specifically request it using an agreed secure channel.

03How we receive and use information

We receive information directly from you, from the organisation you represent, from systems you authorise us to access, and automatically through basic website and security technologies. We use it, as permitted by applicable law, to:

  • respond to enquiries, assess project requirements, prepare proposals and communicate with you;
  • provide, secure, support and improve our software, AI, cybersecurity and IT services;
  • manage client, supplier and business relationships, invoices, records and service requests;
  • operate, maintain and protect the Website, investigate misuse and prevent security incidents;
  • meet legal requirements and establish, exercise or defend legal claims; and
  • send business updates or marketing where permitted and where any required consent has been obtained.

Depending on the context and applicable law, processing may be based on your consent, steps you request before entering a contract, performance of a contract, a legal obligation, or another ground permitted by law. When processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing. Withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful.

04AI services and client information

Where an AI-enabled project processes information, the project agreement should identify the relevant AI components or service providers and the purpose of that processing. We will not use confidential client project data to train a public or general-purpose AI model unless the client has agreed in writing. Client data remains subject to the project agreement, the client's documented instructions and any applicable data-processing agreement.

05When we share personal data

We may share personal data with service providers that help us host and secure the Website, provide email and communications, manage client records, deliver contracted technology services or support our operations. We may also share it with a professional adviser, a competent authority when required by law, a party involved in a business restructuring, or a third party you direct us to use. We limit access to information needed for the relevant task and require appropriate protections where required by law or contract. We do not authorise service providers to use personal data for their own unrelated purposes.

06International processing

Some service providers may store or process information outside the United Arab Emirates. Where personal data is transferred across borders, we use a transfer route and safeguards required by applicable law. For a client project, the agreed contract may specify hosting locations, data residency requirements and approved service providers.

07Cookies and website logs

The Website may use essential cookies or similar technologies to support core functions, protect the site and remember basic preferences. If analytics or other non-essential technologies are used, we will provide the choices or notice required by applicable law. You can also manage cookies in your browser settings, although disabling some cookies may affect site functions. Website and security logs may record technical events needed to operate and protect the Website.

08Retention and security

We retain personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, to provide an agreed service, to meet legal record-keeping requirements or to resolve and defend claims. We then delete or anonymise it where reasonably practicable. The period depends on the type of information and the reason for keeping it. We use technical and organisational safeguards suited to the risks, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

09Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and any legal exceptions, you may ask us for information about personal data we process about you, request correction or completion of inaccurate data, request erasure, request restriction or cessation of processing in qualifying circumstances, request transfer of data where the legal conditions are met, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. You may object to direct marketing at any time. Where applicable law gives you a right to object to a solely automated decision, you may exercise that right as provided by law.

To make a request, email info@luxonneu.com with the subject line “Privacy Request.” Tell us what you are asking for and how we can verify your identity. We may need to retain information to comply with law, protect another person's rights, or establish or defend a legal claim. If a client controls the data, we may refer you to that client or forward the request as appropriate. You may also complain to the competent data-protection authority where applicable.

10Marketing communications

We will send promotional communications only as permitted by applicable law and will obtain consent where required. You can opt out using the unsubscribe method in a message or by emailing info@luxonneu.com. We may still send necessary service, security or legal notices.

11Children and third-party websites

The Website and our business services are intended for business users, not children. We do not knowingly seek personal data directly from children through the Website. The Website may link to third-party sites; this Policy does not govern those sites, so review their own privacy notices before using them.

12Updates and contact

We may update this Policy when our services, data practices or legal requirements change. We will publish the current version on the Website and update the effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Luxon Neu INC FZE at info@luxonneu.com or write to Business Centre, Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates.
